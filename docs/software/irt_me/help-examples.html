The examples in help irt_me, run in full

The examples at the end of help irt_me, on Stata’s masc1 data (five binary items from a mathematics test). The two-parameter logistic model is fit first with irt 2pl and then, identically, with gsem; after gsem the latent() option names the latent variable.

webuse masc1, clear
(Data from De Boeck & Wilson (2004))
irt 2pl q1 q2 q3 q4 q5
